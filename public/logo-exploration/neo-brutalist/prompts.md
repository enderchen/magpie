## 方案1完整提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a production-quality visual identity concept for 纸鹊 Magpie, a simple product that turns uploaded HTML/content files into shareable web pages. This task is LOGO design only, not a website redesign. The user asks for Neo-Brutalist UI style, radical simplicity and memorability.
Target dimensions: 1536 x 1024 landscape identity presentation. One logo idea only per image. Strong hierarchy, crisp typography, purposeful spacing. White background, large principal mark and bilingual wordmark; one restrained bottom row shows the SAME identity in solid black and at small navigation scale. No giant brand guideline document, no cards, no annotations or invented content.
Image 1 is a PALETTE AND EXISTING PRODUCT CONTEXT REFERENCE ONLY. All three pictured previous logos were REJECTED by the user. DO NOT reuse their sailboats, P-with-sail, curving ribbon, typography layout, or their shapes. Reinterpret from scratch in the requested new style.
Palette: flat solid lime #A3E635, ink #25271F, white #FFFFFF only. Neo-brutalist grammar: heavy precise black contours, sturdy block geometry, few forms, optional short hard-edged black offset built into the mark. NO soft shadows, NO gradients, NO grain, NO bevels, NO highlights, NO 3D, NO perspective mockups, NO textures. More reductive than decorative.
Text verbatim: "纸鹊" and "Magpie". Spell P-a-g-e-S-a-i-l, exactly that capitalization. No slogan. No other text. Chinese lettering should be bold, clean, readable modern sans, English bold compact grotesk, optically aligned and balanced with Chinese. Keep the logo as hero and the lettering subordinate. Avoid tiny internal details and disconnected embellishments. The silhouette must hold at 24px. Do not make a collage of competing logo ideas; all repetitions use the identical main mark.
Direction name: 折角页签 / PAGE TAB.
Subject and construction: a single assertive upright page silhouette, almost square with the upper right corner decisively cut and folded. A thick uninterrupted ink outline, lime fill, exactly one white triangular fold; a short solid black offset to the lower right makes it feel like a tactile Neo-Brutalist UI stamp. Reduce to two or three closed shapes, no text lines inside the page, no generic file-icon detailing, no rounded app-icon enclosure. Give it an unusual stepped lower edge as a tiny signature, while keeping the overall silhouette instantly readable. A strong quiet emblem with witty one-corner geometry, not a complex illustration.
Composition: large icon above a centered one-line bilingual wordmark, generous white space. Small monochrome and nav scale specimens below a single black hairline.

Preserve the recognizable logic of 舟 but redraw with brutally simple thick straight strokes, squared-off ends, broad angular outer hull and two deliberately oversized negative-space cuts.
