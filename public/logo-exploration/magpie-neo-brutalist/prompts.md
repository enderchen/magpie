## 折纸成鹊

Use case: logo-brand. Create a refined, memorable and radically SIMPLE product logo concept for the newly renamed Chinese web publishing tool 纸鹊 Magpie. A user uploads a page/file and shares it by link. Brand meaning: paper carries ideas, the magpie delivers them. The approved poetic slogan is 一纸，寄万千.
Style: Neo-Brutalist UI distilled into a real brand mark: decisive thick geometry, bold black mass, balanced negative space, flat punchy lime, sharp purposeful corners. Not a cartoon mascot, not a bird illustration, not a stock icon. Maximum 3-5 large structural forms. Recognizable silhouette when small. Deliberate playful character, professionally balanced.
Target dimensions: 1536 x 1024 landscape. An identity concept sheet for ONE logo only. Main large logo with bilingual wordmark and slogan occupying upper two thirds; small single-color and navigation-size versions of the EXACT SAME mark in restrained lower band separated by one thin line. Generous whitespace. Strong hierarchy, intentional type and purposeful spacing. No website/dashboard design, no extra panels, no option labels, no numbers, no annotations. Do not stretch or warp typography.
Input image 1: reference for PALETTE and bold typography tone ONLY, NOT an edit target. The brand and logo in that image are OBSOLETE. Do not copy its page-arrow graphic, its wording or name. This is an entirely new identity.
Colors: white #FFFFFF, deep ink #25271F, lime #A3E635 ONLY. Solid fills with no gradients, no textures, no blur, no lighting, no highlights, no rendered material, no 3D, no bevels, no soft shadows. Crisp vector-like edges in a raster image.
Exact text only: "纸鹊" "Magpie" "一纸，寄万千". English must spell M-a-g-p-i-e with capital M only. Chinese must be accurate and comfortably readable. Main bilingual name: bold clean contemporary sans serif, Chinese optically balanced with English. Slogan much smaller, regular weight, airy. Avoid old names 纸鹊, Magpie, Paperjay entirely. No additional slogan or English translation.
Avoid: intricate feathers, tiny toes, multiple eyes, swallows/doves/owls/eagles, round app-icon enclosures, hearts, envelopes covered in lines, trees/branches, stars/sparkles, motion lines, decorative badges. The mark must work in monochrome and without its wordmark. Do not add a separate cartoon beside a letter symbol. A single integrated emblem is essential.
Distinct direction: 折纸成鹊 / FOLDED MAGPIE.
Design an angular magpie silhouette built from one folded page: a strong long tapered tail angled down-left, a compact head with a short pointed beak facing right, and one broad lime folded wing. The white negative-space fold is the essential memorable detail connecting paper to bird. Reduce the body to just two ink planes plus one lime wing, no eye needed, no feet. Asymmetric clever geometry, a solid chunky silhouette, no many little triangles, no realistic origami rendering. It should feel like a bird cut from one thick piece of paper, not a paper airplane.
Composition: emblem large on left and bilingual name to right on two aligned lines, slogan directly underneath; generous margins. Lower specimens repeat this same emblem, one ink-only, one small with name.
