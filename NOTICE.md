PageSail is an independent derivative of JPage (https://github.com/code2rich/jpage).
The original copyright and MIT permission notice are retained unchanged in LICENSE.

The PageSail repository is https://github.com/enderchen/pagesail.
The original JPage logos and their asset notice remain in public/jpage_logo for provenance;
the PageSail interface uses its own PS text mark instead.

Legacy CLI names, JPAGE environment variables, session/storage keys and MCP resource
identifiers remain for compatibility. They do not identify the operator of this fork.

Historical documentation, migrations, templates and bundled upstream integrations may
refer to JPage. User content and database records are not rewritten by this rebrand.

This source checkout is marked private to prevent accidental npm publication.
GitHub visibility is unaffected by the package.json private flag.
